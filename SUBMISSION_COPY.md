# CONFLUX CORE — Contest submission copy

**Title:** CONFLUX CORE — One Reactor. Four Minds.

**Cover image:** `static/cover.png` (original key art; it isn't a gameplay capture, and real screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/`)

**Project URL:** https://conflux-core.onrender.com

**Description:**

> CONFLUX CORE is a real-time co-op puzzle for 1–4 players that runs in any browser: no login, no install. Your crew controls four reactor stations linked in a ring around a collapsing energy core. Turning your station forward pushes your teammate's station backward, so nobody can solve their part alone: you have to talk, plan the order, and avoid undoing each other's work.
>
> Create a room and share a 5-letter code or link; friends join from a phone or laptop in seconds, even mid-game. Playing alone? Quick Play pairs you with an AI crewmate who handles its own stations while you handle yours. Three escalating rounds race the clock, and every round has a *par*, the fewest moves possible (computed by a provably optimal solver), so crews compete on speed and precision, not luck. Hints are there for new players at a cost.
>
> Live symbols on every ring, an in-game briefing, chat, sound, keyboard shortcuts, a cinematic arena plus an optional real-time 3D view, and automatic reconnection make it easy to pick up and hard to put down.

**Short version (if a character limit applies):**

> A real-time browser co-op puzzle for 1–4 players: every turn you make pushes a teammate's ring the other way. Share a room code, coordinate in chat, and stabilize the reactor in three timed rounds, or play with an AI crewmate. No login, no install.
