STABILIZATION BRIEFING

How to play

  1. Goal: turn every station until its current symbol matches its target. All four aligned = core stable.
  2. The twist: turning a station + also turns the next station clockwise − (and vice versa).
    AETHER+1⟶EMBER−1⟶PULSE⟶PRISM↺ back to AETHER
  3. Teamwork: each player controls only their own stations. Talk in chat, plan the order, and don't undo your teammate's work.
  4. Score: finish fast and near par (the fewest possible moves). Hints help but cost 75 points. Three rounds, each harder and shorter.
